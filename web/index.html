Talk to an AI voice agent in a Vonage Video call

Your browser and a Pipecat agent join the same Vonage Video session. Deepgram transcribes you, OpenAI writes the reply, and ElevenLabs speaks it.

You are talking to an AI, not a person. Its answers and its voice are AI-generated, and the answers can be wrong. Your microphone audio travels through Vonage Video to Deepgram, and the text of the conversation goes to OpenAI and ElevenLabs, for the length of the call. This demo does not record or store anything.

Build your agent, one stage at a time

A speech-to-speech model makes these choices for you. Here each stage is its own service, so you choose how the agent listens, thinks, speaks, and takes turns.

Echo mode (AGENT_MODE=echo): the agent beeps, then repeats your microphone. No AI providers, so there is nothing to choose.
